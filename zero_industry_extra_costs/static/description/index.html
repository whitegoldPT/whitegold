Overview

The Odoo system standardly enables you to specify service items in the BOM, but when you create a manufacturing Order, the service items are automatically excluded, and therefore adding service items in the BOM is meaningless. All industrial activities also need to bear a share of Variable OverHead on the product, including the cost of “labor, energy, and many other industrial expenses...” For all of the above, we have provided a set of solutions for this purpose, as explained below

Manufacturing Variable OverHead Costs With Accounting Entry

Overview

KIT Products, such as “meal, table, computer,... etc.,” when sold, whether from point of sale, sales, or even e-commerce, when sold or returned from the same previous sales screens, the materials that make up the KIT Product appear in the Stock Moves, but the KIT Product itself that is required does not appear. Assembling it or returning its components to and from stock. Therefore, it is not possible for the warehouses to analyze the movements of disbursement or return of collected items in such cases as the previous ones Also, when the warehouse exchange is printed, the required quantity of the KIT Product does not appear, but the quantity of the materials composing it appears. How can the person in charge of the warehouse know the quantity required to be assembled, and how can the customer sign the Picking Delivery or Picking Recepts"Returns" if it does not indicate the quantity spent or the quantity returned!? In addition to the above, the Odoo system does not automatically update the cost of the KIT Product, but the updating process requires manual intervention But with this application we have provided solutions to all the problems mentioned above.

There is an error in the accounting entry created from Delivery Picking and it has been corrected. When selling an KIT Product , the cost of sales "Stock Output Account" for the cost of the KIT Product is issued according to the accounts related to raw materials, but the correction has been made to the account related to the KIT Product ""
